### 1. Google Search Console (CRITICAL)
- [ ] Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console/
- [ ] Sign in with Google account
- [ ] Click "Add Property" → "URL prefix"
- [ ] Enter: `https://nexora-ai-flax.vercel.app`
