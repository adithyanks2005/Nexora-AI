This is called **temporary de-indexing** and is **REVERSIBLE**.

## 🎯 5-Minute Fix (Do This RIGHT NOW)

### Step 1: Go to Google Search Console
**URL:** https://search.google.com/search-console/

**If you haven't set it up yet:**
1. Click "Add Property"
2. Choose "URL prefix"
3. Enter: `https://nexora-ai-flax.vercel.app`
4. Verify using "HTML tag" (already in your site)
5. Click "Verify"

### Step 2: Check for Issues
1. Look at **Overview** page
2. Check for any red errors or warnings
3. Look at **Pages** section (left sidebar → Indexing → Pages)
4. See "Why pages aren't indexed"

**Common issues you might see:**
- "Server error (5xx)" ← This was your problem!
- "Crawled - currently not indexed"
- "Discovered - currently not indexed"

### Step 3: Request Re-indexing
1. Click **URL Inspection** (left sidebar, top)
2. Enter: `https://nexora-ai-flax.vercel.app`
3. Click **Test Live URL**
4. Wait for test to complete (30-60 seconds)
5. If test passes, click **REQUEST INDEXING**
6. Confirm the request

**This tells Google:** "My site is fixed, please crawl it again!"

### Step 4: Submit Your Sitemap
1. Click **Sitemaps** (left sidebar → Indexing → Sitemaps)
2. In the "Add a new sitemap" box, type: `sitemap.xml`
3. Click **Submit**

**This gives Google your site structure**

### Step 5: Create Fresh Backlinks
Google re-discovers sites faster when it finds links to them.

**Post TODAY on:**
- Twitter/X: "Launched MediCura AI - free AI healthcare assistant"
- LinkedIn: Share your project with hashtags
- Reddit: r/SideProject - "Show HN: AI Healthcare Assistant"
- Dev.to: Write a quick post about your project

**Each link helps Google find your site again**
