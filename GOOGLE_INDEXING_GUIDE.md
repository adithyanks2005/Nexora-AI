   - Click "Continue"

3. **Verify Ownership:**

   - Select "HTML tag" method
   - Click "Verify"
   - Should say "Ownership verified" ✅

4. **Submit Your Sitemap:**
   - In Google Search Console, go to "Sitemaps" (left sidebar)
   - Enter: `sitemap.xml`
   - Click "Submit"

5. **Request Indexing:**

   - Click "Test Live URL"
   - Click "Request Indexing"

## Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue: "Site can't be reached" in Google Search Console
**Solution:** Make sure your site is actually online and not showing "Bad Gateway" errors. Fix deployment issues first.

### Issue: "Submitted URL not found (404)"
**Solution:** Verify the URL is correct and accessible. Check for typos.

### Issue: "Crawled but not indexed"
**Solution:** 
- Improve content quality and uniqueness
- Add more internal links
- Get external backlinks
- Wait longer (can take time for new sites)

### Issue: "Discovered but not crawled"
**Solution:**
- Be patient, Google will crawl eventually
- Request indexing manually
- Ensure robots.txt isn't blocking crawlers
